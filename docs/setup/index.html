CHOMPI firmwares • setup

Set up your CHOMPI

Choose which firmware goes on which key, then write them all to your CHOMPI in one go. No card reader, no files to copy.

Built on the multi-firmware launcher by @sfaber02. The firmwares are made by people in the CHOMPI community; each one below links to its release.

  • 3 steps
  • launcher 1.5
  • Chrome or Edge

DemoNo CHOMPI here: this page is talking to a pretend one in your browser, so nothing is written anywhere. Reload to start over.

Use at your own riskThis is an unofficial community project, not made or supported by CHOMPI Club, and it comes without any warranty. It writes to your SD card and replaces what is on the keys you change, so back up your card first. If something goes wrong, switch CHOMPI off and on.

Needs launcher 1.5. Step 1 tells you if yours is older and how to update it. More firmwares join the list as their authors add them; here's how to add yours.

  1. 1Connectnot connected

    Switch CHOMPI on so its keys are breathing, plug it into this computer, then connect. Your browser will ask to allow MIDI; say yes. After that, this page connects by itself.

    This CHOMPI's launcher can only take one firmware at a time. This page needs the new launcher:

    1. Download CHOMPI.bin, the new launcher.
    2. Switch CHOMPI off, take out the SD card and put it in your computer.
    3. Replace the CHOMPI.bin at the top level of the card with the downloaded one. Leave the FIRMWARE folder alone.
    4. Put the card back and switch CHOMPI on. A rainbow shows while it installs the new launcher.

    Until then, this launcher can still take one firmware at a time from the firmware page.

  2. 2Choose firmwares

    Each white key on CHOMPI can hold one firmware. Tap a key, then choose what goes on it. Or tap Put on key on a firmware below. With launcher 1.6 or newer, CHOMPI's keys light up in the colours you see here.

    Not connected yet.

    Standard setup: TAPE on 1, TEMPO on 2, WAVE on 3, USB storage on 15. Other keys stay as they are.
